# Code of Conduct

Rivelle is committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help others participate.
- Keep project spaces focused on building and supporting Rivelle.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads, reject contributions or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this policy is violated. Serious reports can be sent privately through the repository's security reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and as promptly as practical.

This policy applies to all Rivelle project spaces and to public representation of the project.
